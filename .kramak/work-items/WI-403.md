## Intent
Transform the Recruiter JD Matcher into a high-converting candidate qualification terminal. Recruiters can paste their JD or click role presets to instantly see Akanksha's compatibility score, 4-domain competency radar, verified STAR evidence, and export a ready-to-share candidate dossier.

## Key Files
- `src/components/JdMatcher.astro`: Component handling client-side JD analysis, 4-domain fit radar, STAR story suggestions, and candidate dossier export.

## Specification
1. In `src/components/JdMatcher.astro`:
   - Role Preset Bar: 4 one-click presets for common target roles with pre-populated JD text.
   - Real-time debounced matcher (300ms) with keyword dictionary covering 50+ AI engineering terms across 4 categories:
     - Domain A: Agentic AI & Multi-Agent Orchestration (Google ADK, LangGraph, CrewAI, MCP, tool use)
     - Domain B: Graph RAG & Advanced Vector Search (Graph RAG, FAISS, Chroma, Neo4j, Hybrid Search)
     - Domain C: LLM Infra, Serving & Observability (GPT-4, Llama-3, AWS Bedrock, FastAPI, Docker, LangSmith)
     - Domain D: Enterprise ML Reliability & Evaluation (RAGAS, Python, CI/CD, incident response, 98% uptime)
   - Visual Scoring Display:
     - Overall match percentage with animated SVG circular ring.
     - 4-Domain SVG Radar / Spider Polygon Chart dynamically updated according to domain fit.
   - Matched Keywords Badges: Interactive chips showing matched terms categorized by domain.
   - STAR Story Recommender: Expandable accordion with concrete STAR stories based on matched skills (e.g. Samsung SDS 92% RAG story, Google ADK 60% effort reduction story, HCLTech 500+ incident resolution story).
   - Candidate Match Dossier Exporter: One-click export modal / copy button generating clean markdown dossier ready for Slack, Email, or hiring manager review.

## Constraints
- Pure Astro + vanilla JS (zero server dependency; works completely statically on Cloudflare Workers edge).
- Accessible and responsive on mobile and desktop.

## Verification
- `pnpm run build` passes with zero errors.
