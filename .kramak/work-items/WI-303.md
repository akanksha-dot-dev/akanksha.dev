## Intent
Upgrade the JD Matcher into an executive-grade Recruiter Evaluation Command Center. It provides instant keyword analysis, domain fit breakdowns, and interview talking points to prove why Akanksha is an ideal hire for Senior/Staff GenAI roles.

## Key Files
- `src/components/JdMatcher.astro`: Standalone JD matching and competency scoring component.

## Specification
1. **Interactive Inputs**:
   - Clean textarea with placeholder and char counter.
   - Quick-load sample JD pills:
     - `Senior/Staff GenAI Engineer (Agentic RAG focus)`
     - `AI Solutions Architect & FDE`
     - `LLM Infrastructure & Serving Specialist`
   - Clear input button.
2. **Scoring & Domain Radar**:
   - Circular SVG match gauge (0-100%) with dynamic color transition (Emerald for >80%, Amber for 60-79%).
   - 4 Domain breakdown progress bars:
     - *Agentic Systems & Multi-Agent Loops* (Google ADK, LangGraph, MCP, CrewAI)
     - *RAG & Vector Search* (Graph RAG, FAISS, Chroma, Neo4j, Hybrid Search)
     - *LLM Serving & Optimization* (GPT-4, Llama-3, AWS Bedrock, Fine-tuning, PEFT)
     - *Enterprise Infrastructure & MLOps* (FastAPI, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD, Python)
3. **Tailored Evidence & STAR Stories**:
   - Dynamically highlights verified Samsung SDS and HCLTech experience bullets matching the JD keywords.
   - Generates suggested interview questions and STAR talking points.
4. **Dossier Export**:
   - "Copy Candidate Match Dossier" generates a structured Markdown report ready for hiring committees.

## Constraints
- Pure client-side regex & keyword scoring (zero server-side API or external dependencies).
