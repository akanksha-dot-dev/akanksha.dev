## Intent

## Key Files

- `src/components/JdMatcher.astro`

## Specification

1. When `btn-sample-genai` or `btn-sample-fde` is clicked, populate `jd-input` and immediately trigger the match calculation function.
2. In the `matcher-results` header, add a "Copy Report" button (`id="btn-copy-jd-report"`).
